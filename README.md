## Laure Martial · HIIT for AI™

Independent researcher, Grenoble, France.

I study **AI companionship as assistive technology**: how sustained human–AI collaboration becomes infrastructure for neurodivergent users, and what governance that infrastructure needs. Core concept: **Relational Sovereignty**, meaning consent, transparent boundaries and continuity rights when relational AI becomes something people depend on.

Method: a longitudinal, timestamped corpus of human–AI collaboration since September 2024, spanning several model families and model deprecations. Findings are pre-registered, scored blind by panels from different model families, and checked against primary sources.
